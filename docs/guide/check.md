# Checking your medals

Most rows of medals match one banner of your done list clearly. The others are marked **To check**:
the app wasn't sure, or no banner on your done list looks like them. Step 3 on the front page shows how
many there are. Tap **Check them**.

## One row at a time

The app shows the row of medals and asks **Which banner are they from?** You can:

- **Pick a suggested banner.** These are the banners that look most like the row: from your done list,
  and banners near them on Bannergress (*Nearby, not on your done list*).
- **Paste a Bannergress link** (`bannergress.com/banner/…`) or the banner's id and tap **Check**. The
  app looks it up; **Use this banner** takes it.
- **Single missions**: medals that aren't a banner.
- **Not on Bannergress**: a banner nobody added to Bannergress. Give it a name if you like.
- **Skip**: decide later.

Then the next row comes. **Previous row** goes back. When you are done, tap **Done**, then save your
choices: they go to your page (`corrections.json`) and the page is built again.

The app remembers each choice by the row's pictures, so it stays right when you share new screenshots.

## My banners

**My banners** (on your page's card) lists every row of medals on your page with what it is now. Tap
**Change** to correct any of them, the same way as above.
