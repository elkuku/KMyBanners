# Privacy

KMyBanners has no server and no account of its own. Everything goes between your phone, GitHub and
Bannergress.

## What is public

Your page's repository `ingress-banners` is **public**, and so is the page. Anyone can see:

- your agent name;
- the screenshots of your mission medals and the picture made from them;
- your Bannergress done list;
- where you did banners, on the map;
- your choices for the rows you checked.

Your GitHub name is part of the page's address.

## What stays on your phone

The app keeps its sign-in to GitHub and to Bannergress in its own storage on your phone. **Sign out**
in the settings removes the GitHub sign-in.

GitHub's access is for your public repositories. You can take it back any time on GitHub:
**Settings → Applications → Authorized OAuth Apps → KMyBanners → Revoke**.

## Removing your page

Delete the repository on GitHub: open `ingress-banners`, **Settings**, at the bottom **Delete this
repository**. The page is gone a few minutes later.

KMyBanners is not affiliated with Niantic Spatial, Ingress or Bannergress.
